## The name

**MicroCortex** — *micro* (microcontrollers, $5 chips) + *cortex* (the part of the
brain that does the thinking). "A cortex for your $5 chip."

- Identical in English and Spanish ✓
- Sounds like tech, not like a meme (or a tire) ✓
- Two syllables + two syllables, easy to say on video ✓
- `microllm` (repo), `microcortex` (org/handle), `microcortex.dev` (domain) all align
- Sign-off: *"In memory of Johnny."* (von Neumann's nickname — our internal homages)
